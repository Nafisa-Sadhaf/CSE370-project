How This Is Calculated

Total Clients

Counted live from the CLIENT table — any client whose AdvisorID matches this advisor is included.

Avg Portfolio Growth

For every asset: (CurrentValue − PurchasePrice) ÷ PurchasePrice × 100. Then averaged across all assets of all clients under this advisor.

Total AUM

Assets Under Management — sum of (Quantity × CurrentValue) for every portfolio asset belonging to this advisor's clients.
